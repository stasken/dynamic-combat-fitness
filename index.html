First time? Here's how to enter the building

  1. Park in the gravel lot behind the building — the front roller door is loading only.
  2. Enter through the black steel side door marked "DYNAMIC." Buzzer is on the left.
  3. Head down the stairs — the front desk is straight ahead under the neon sign.
  4. Tell the desk it's your first session. We'll set you up with gloves and wraps, no charge.
